# Privacy Policy - Blendfall

_Last updated: July 2026_

Blendfall does **not collect, store, or share any personal data**.

The app runs entirely offline. Your game progress - which levels you've solved, your
star ratings, your Blitz best scores, your remaining hints, your chosen theme, block
shape and language - is saved **only on your own device** using the system's standard
local storage, and is never transmitted anywhere.

- We do **not** use analytics, advertising, or tracking of any kind.
- We do **not** use third-party services or SDKs, except the store's own billing service
  (see "In-app purchases" below).
- We do **not** request any device permissions.
- We do **not** have user accounts or sign-in.

**In-app purchases:** Blendfall offers optional one-time purchases (for example,
unlocking all levels, theme packs, or hint refills). Payments are processed entirely by
**Google Play** (on Android) or the **App Store** (on iOS). We never see, receive, or
store your payment details. The store only tells the app which products you own so it
can unlock them; that ownership record is managed by the store and cached on your device.

Because the app collects no data, there is nothing for us to access, share, delete, or
export. Deleting the app removes the locally stored progress from your device. Purchases
can be restored on a new device through the store ("Restore purchases" in the app).

**Children's privacy:** Blendfall is suitable for all ages and collects no data from
anyone, including children.

If you have any questions about this policy, contact us at: **rongoapp2026@gmail.com**
