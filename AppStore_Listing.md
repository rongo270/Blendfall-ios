SMART HINTS

WHY YOU'LL LOVE IT

• Up to three stars on every level

• Works fully offline — no ads, no sign-up, no tracking

The first 150 levels, Blitz mode and 30 hints are completely free. One optional purchase unlocks all 300 levels plus exclusive themes and shapes, forever — no subscriptions, ever.

Slide the blocks. Blend the colors. Feel the fall.

## 5. App Privacy (the "Data Not Collected" questionnaire)

In App Store Connect → **App Privacy** → answer:
- **Do you collect data from this app?** → **No**

That's it. Blendfall stores progress only on-device (UserDefaults), has no analytics, no
ads, no third-party SDKs. In-app purchases are processed entirely by Apple (StoreKit) —
that does not count as the app collecting data.
